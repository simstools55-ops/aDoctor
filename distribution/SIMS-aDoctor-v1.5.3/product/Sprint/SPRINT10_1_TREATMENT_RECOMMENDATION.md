# Sprint10.1 — Treatment Recommendation Engine

## Included

- treatment recommendation contract
- Composite Diagnosis mapping
- Writer, Creator, Merge, Observe, Follow-Up, and None targets
- treatment scope and prohibited actions
- Winner Query and full-rewrite safeguards
- new-article permission safeguard
- monitoring requirements
- referral request factory
- Medical Record history
- idempotent event
- contract, unit, integration, and regression tests

## Excluded

- treatment execution
- automatic publication
- automatic redirect, delete, or noindex
- user-facing report generation
