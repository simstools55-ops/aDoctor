# Treatment History Comparison Diagnosis

Doctor compares the pre-treatment baseline with post-treatment checkpoints.

## Outcomes

- Treatment Success
- Improvement Failure
- Post-Improvement Worsening
- Mixed Treatment Response
- Follow-Up Required

## Safety

Doctor does not automatically restore an old article version.
Post-improvement worsening is referred to Writer with a directive to compare the previous
changes and present partial correction or restoration options.
