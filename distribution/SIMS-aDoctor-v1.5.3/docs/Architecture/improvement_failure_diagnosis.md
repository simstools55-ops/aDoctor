# Improvement Failure Diagnosis

The engine combines treatment response and article health changes.

## Outcomes

- Treatment Effective
- No Measurable Effect
- Post-Treatment Worsening
- Possible Wrong Treatment Direction
- Recurrent Failure
- Insufficient Follow-Up

## Wrong treatment direction

This is suspected when the effect score is negative and multiple core metrics,
or a core metric plus Vital Score, deteriorate together.

## Safety

LOW_SAMPLE and short follow-up cases are not confirmed as failure.
Doctor never rolls back an article automatically.
