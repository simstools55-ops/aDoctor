# Vital Sign Formulas v1

These formulas are initial, conservative, and intentionally simple.

Important rules:

- Every score is 0–100.
- Higher always means healthier.
- Missing inputs produce `UNAVAILABLE`, not guessed values.
- LOW_SAMPLE evidence reduces confidence and may apply a score penalty.
- Competition Resilience and Content Integrity remain unavailable until SERP and
  Article Snapshot observations are implemented.
- Formula changes must be versioned in Knowledge.
