# Sprint8.1 — Batch Doctor Foundation

## Included

- batch request and result contracts
- up to 500 items by policy
- per-article Case isolation
- priority scoring
- priority-ordered execution
- continue-after-failure behavior
- resume from previous result
- retry limits
- aggregate referral and follow-up counts
- contract, unit, integration, and regression tests

## Excluded

- persistent queue
- cron or night scheduler
- parallel worker execution
- SBM batch-import UI
- email or notification delivery
