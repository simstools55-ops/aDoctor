# Product Responsibility Boundary

- Doctor never edits or dispatches directly to another specialist.
- Writer never creates a separate new-article identity unless SBM issues a Creator path.
- Creator never silently edits existing articles; it returns follow-up referrals.
- Merge never directly deletes, redirects, noindexes, or publishes.
- SBM does not replace specialist SEO/editorial judgement; it validates, stores, routes, and measures.
- Shared is the only canonical source for common contracts, enums, and cross-product rules.
- SBM may collect and summarize site-wide performance evidence, but does not make the specialist Algorithm Impact diagnosis.
- Doctor owns Algorithm Impact interpretation and integrated diagnostic judgement; Google update information remains one Evidence class among others.
- Shared defines the cross-product evidence and routing boundary, not Doctor-specific scoring or causation logic.
