# Position Opportunity Diagnosis

Position Opportunity uses impressions, average position, query concentration,
competition resilience, and content integrity.

## Key outcomes

- High Position Opportunity
- Position Opportunity
- Query-Focused Opportunity
- Winner Query Protected
- Low Visibility or Misalignment
- Healthy Position
- Insufficient Data

## Boundary

A position-only diagnosis does not authorize a new article or content deletion.
Those decisions require intent, cannibalization, and composite diagnosis.
