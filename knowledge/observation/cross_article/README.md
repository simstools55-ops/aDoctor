# Cross-Article Observation

Cross-Article Observation compares the diagnosed article with other articles on the same site.

It records:

- shared queries
- query-overlap ratio
- title similarity
- intent similarity
- clicks, impressions, CTR, and position for each article
- dominant article
- possible cannibalization
- merge suitability
- distinct-intent/new-article opportunity

Doctor never merges, deletes, or creates an article in this layer.
